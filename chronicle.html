What has been happening

28 October 2026 — the site is drafted

The book, the film and the search for it are set out on one page, in the GYR-O format: plain answers, every source previewed, and the detail. Two things shaped it. The question of record, whether the film existed and where it went, is kept apart from the question of what kind of explosion it was, because the search can only answer the first. And every place where the story as told differs from a published source is listed in a table when it is found, not when it is resolved: the death toll, the ships, the year of Vogel’s article, the title of the Gadget document, Roosevelt’s whereabouts.

The most useful thing found while drafting was a 2007 radio program whose description mentions “a film, purporting to be a simulation of the Port Chicago explosion”. It is the only outside mention of a film so far, and the recording has not yet been heard.

Still open

  • The film. No copy found; the archives are to be asked, and Vogel’s captured web pages searched.
  • The 2007 recording. What Vogel says about the “simulation” film.
  • The Gadget document’s title. Pound or ton, from the copy itself.
  • The Navy’s denial. Where and when it was made.
  • Roosevelt. The FDR Library’s entry for 17 July 1944, and Herndon’s evidence.
